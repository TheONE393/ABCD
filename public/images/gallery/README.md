# Lab Gallery Images (`public/images/gallery/`)

Add any photos here (e.g. lab retreats, group events, bench work, microscopy celebrations, conferences).

## How it works:
1. **Drop your photos here**:
   - Supported formats: `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.webp`, `.avif`
   - Example names: `group-lunch-2026.jpg`, `annual-symposium.jpg`, `lab-bench-session.jpg`
2. **Automatic Detection**:
   - The slideshow on the People page will automatically detect and include any images placed in this folder.
   - The slideshow auto-rotates in a loop, pauses on hover, and includes previous/next controls and navigation dots.
3. **Removing or Replacing**:
   - Simply delete or replace images in this folder at any time.
